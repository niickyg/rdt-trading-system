  dishonest.

- **Did not enable AUTO_TRADE or touch credentials.** Paper-only respected.
- **Explicitly NOT pursuing the SaaS / signal-service path** as a way to "make
  the bot profitable." Revenue from selling signals is not the bot being
  profitable; the mission's benchmark is beating SPY with trading P&L.
