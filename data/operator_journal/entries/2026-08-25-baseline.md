## 2. What I found

Four independent lines of evidence, all pointing the same way (full detail in
`POST_MORTEM_RRS.md`):

1. **Live account: -61.5% TWR vs SPY +11.3%.** Decisive and unambiguous.
2. **In-sample optimization** (`data/optimization/optimization_2025-12-29.json`): best of
   180 param sets → Sharpe **0.11**, profit factor 1.29, win rate 38%. Optimized/overfit
   and *still* ~noise.
3. **Walk-forward** (`CLAUDE.md`): best config ~**3.4% annualized** — far below SPY.
4. **The project's own Kelly analysis** (`ACTIONABLE_100X_STRATEGY.md`): explicitly
   computes **Kelly = -0.02 (negative)** and states increasing size "increases risk of
   ruin without improving returns." The -61.5% drawdown is that prediction realized,
   after the AGGRESSIVE 3%/trade deployment in `DEPLOYMENT_SUMMARY.md`.

Two secondary findings:
- **Docs-vs-reality gap:** `CLAUDE.md` claims a $25k account `DUP995654`; the connected
  live account is a ~$50-start micro account now at $5. Documentation is not tracking the
  account actually being traded.
- **Strategic drift:** the newest commits are SaaS/signal-service product work; the "path
  to 100%" doc relies 40–60% on *selling* the signals, not trading them. Classic tell of
  a strategy with no tradable edge.

## 3. Hypothesis (single, falsifiable)

**None survives contact with the evidence.** Across in-sample optimization, walk-forward,
the authors' own Kelly math, and six months of live paper results, the RRS-at-this-
granularity strategy shows no positive, cost-aware edge. There is no parameter hypothesis
worth testing, because 180 optimized parameter sets already produced Sharpe 0.11 / negative
Kelly. A knob does not turn a negative edge positive.

The only honest hypotheses left are structural (different holding period / instrument /
signal with an articulable reason an edge would *persist*) — none of which I can
responsibly assert or test in a single first session, and none of which the current codebase
is evidence *for*.
