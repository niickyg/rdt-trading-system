## 3. Evidence (the decisive comparison)

Window: **2024-07-26 → 2026-07-28** (~2.0 yrs, 510 trading days), $25K.

| Config (best = C, RDT filters) | Total return | Annualized | Max DD | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bot C — **gross** | +9.81% ($2,454) | 4.8% | ~2.8% | 280 |
| Bot C — **net of costs** | **+7.11% ($1,777)** | **3.5%** | ~3.0% | 280 |
| **SPY buy-and-hold** | **+39.30% ($9,825)** | **~18.0%** | ~18.8% | 1 |

- Transaction costs removed **~27% of the gross return** (gross 4.8% → net 3.5%
  annualized). Ignoring them materially overstated performance.
- Net of costs, the bot captured **under 20% of SPY's return** over the same
  period. It did so with ~280 trades vs one buy-and-hold.
- The bot's **only** advantage is drawdown (~3% vs ~18.8%). It is a
  low-volatility, low-return product — **not** a SPY-beater.

Caveat (stated honestly): this 2-year window was a strong, near-monotonic SPY
bull market, which flatters buy-and-hold and gives a long/short momentum book
little room to add value. The bot's low drawdown would matter more in a crash
(none occurred in-window). But the mandate's bar is beating SPY on P&L, and on
that bar the result is a clear, wide **miss**, in every window tested.

## 5. Recommendation (for the human owner)

1. **Do NOT deploy the aggressive risk profile or the 100X plan.** Raising risk /
   adding leverage/options/crypto does not create edge; it scales the path to
   ruin. Selling signals is not the trading mandate. Revert the AGGRESSIVE `.env`
   drift toward conservative defaults (1% risk, 10% position, 2–3% daily loss).
2. **Stop trusting gross backtests.** Make `BACKTEST_COSTS=1` (or an even more
   conservative cost assumption) the default basis for any go/no-go decision.
3. **Decide the strategic question** (it is yours, not the operator's):
   - (A) Reframe the bot as a low-vol diversifier and benchmark it honestly
     against market-neutral/low-vol peers — but accept it fails *this* mandate; or
   - (B) Use the `research/` factor framework to search for a genuinely different
     edge that survives walk-forward **net of costs** before risking capital; or
   - (C) Wind down the trading ambition if further evidence keeps saying no edge
     beats the index net of costs.
4. **Root-cause the 99% short bias and the dead scanner** before any live paper
   run is trusted — the signal generator is currently producing nonsense, and
   there is no realized-P&L record to check it against.

My recommendation leans **(B) with a hard honesty gate**: one more session should
