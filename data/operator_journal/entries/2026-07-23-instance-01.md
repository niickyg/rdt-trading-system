## 3. Verdict

**On the evidence available, this strategy does not clear the bar the mandate sets
(positive P&L net of honest costs, beating SPY buy-and-hold).** It fails on two independent
grounds:

1. **No edge in the optimistic case.** The best *in-sample* result is ~3.4% annualized. SPY
   buy-and-hold over the same era returned ~16.5%+ annualized. A day-trading momentum system
   that, at its curve-fit best, delivers a fifth of the index's return while incurring
   per-trade slippage/commissions and full-time attention is a value-destroying activity
   versus the trivial alternative of holding SPY.
2. **No evidence at all in the real case.** 2 tracked outcomes, a $5 account, and 4.5 months
   of dormancy. There is nothing here that could be called a track record.

This is precisely the situation the mandate says to name plainly: *"If the evidence keeps
saying no strategy works, say so in the journal and recommend escalation or wind-down."*

## 4. Recommendation to the human (the one decision that matters)

**Do not invest more engineering effort into tuning this strategy until a threshold
question is answered:** is there any out-of-sample, cost-inclusive evidence that RRS
day-trading beats SPY buy-and-hold? Concretely, I recommend, in priority order:

1. **Decide the account discrepancy.** Point the operator at a real, funded paper account
   (the claimed DUP995654 or a fresh $25K paper account) — otherwise every future instance
   is flying blind with no way to measure anything. Nothing else is worth doing first.
2. **Run a genuine forward paper test with honest costs** (commission + realistic slippage)
   and *automatic outcome tracking* until **≥100 closed trades** accumulate. Pre-register
   the success criterion in the mandate (already done): beat SPY over the same window. Until
   that sample exists, all backtest numbers in the repo are curve-fit noise.
3. **If the forward test underperforms SPY (the likely outcome given §2d), wind the trading
   thesis down** and repurpose the (genuinely well-built) infrastructure — or accept that
   the honest recommendation for a $25K account is: **buy SPY and stop day-trading.**

I did **not** implement any of these because (1) requires a human decision about which
account to use and consent to spend on a long forward test, and because acting on a trading
system without a verified mandate and a funded account would be irresponsible.
