## 1. Assessment — the honest headline

The mission is: *positive P&L, net of honest costs, beating SPY buy-and-hold.*
I checked whether that has ever been measured. It has not.

- `scripts/run_walkforward_v2.py` (the canonical 2-year, 3-way backtest that
  produced the numbers quoted in `CLAUDE.md`) models **zero** commissions and
  **zero** slippage, and computes **no** benchmark.
- `scripts/run_walkforward.py`: same — no costs, no benchmark.
- `scripts/run_backtest.py`: references a 0.1% slippage but, by its own comments,
  only "documents" it in reporting — it does **not** apply it to fills.

So every headline figure in the repo is a **gross, pre-cost, benchmark-free**
number. Taken at face value, the best config ("C) RDT Filters") returns
**+6.9% (~+$1,716) over ~21 months, ~3.4% annualized** on $25k.

I computed SPY buy-and-hold over the **exact same window** (2024-02-01 →
2025-11-28) fresh via yfinance (adjusted close):

| | RDT Config C (gross, pre-cost) | SPY buy-and-hold |
|---|---|---|
| Total return | +6.9% (+$1,716) | **+42.7% (+$10,682)** |
| Annualized | +3.4% | **+21.5%** |
| Round-trip trades | 279 | 1 |
| Ending value on $25k | ~$26,716 | ~$35,682 |

**The strategy loses to one-click buy-and-hold by ~36 points over the period it
was tuned on — before costs. Deducting costs widens the gap.** This is the
"evidence says no" situation the mandate tells me to surface plainly.

I also noted the repo's `*_100X.md` / `WEALTH_*.md` docs propose closing the gap
via risk inflation (3% risk, 6% daily loss, 20% positions), leverage, and
**selling signals to other people**. Per the mandate these are anti-goals — none
is the bot honestly becoming profitable. The committed `.env.example` still has
conservative defaults (good), but a human should verify the *live* `.env` has
not drifted to the aggressive profile that `DEPLOYMENT_SUMMARY.md` records.

## 2. Change made this session

One focused, reviewable change in service of the mission's actual criterion:
**make net-of-cost, benchmark-relative return measurable** in the canonical
backtest, instead of leaving the headline metric unmeasured.

`scripts/run_walkforward_v2.py`:
- Added `ROUND_TRIP_COST_PCT = 0.0010` — a documented, conservative placeholder
  (0.10% of entry notional per round trip) with a note to calibrate to the real
  broker/universe.
- Added `round_trip_cost(results)` — sums estimated execution cost from each
  trade's entry notional (`entry_price * shares`).
- Added `spy_buy_hold_return(spy_data, results)` — SPY buy-and-hold $ P&L over
  the **same** walk-forward windows, aggregated the same way as strategy P&L.
- Added a "HONEST COST + BENCHMARK ADJUSTMENT" report block: gross vs. net P&L
  per config, SPY buy-and-hold over the same windows, and an explicit verdict
  line stating whether any config beats buy-and-hold net of costs.

This changes **reporting only**. It does not touch `risk/`, trade logic,
execution, credentials, or `AUTO_TRADE`. It cannot place a trade.

## 4. Recommendation

1. **Accept the headline honestly:** as currently implemented and measured, the
   intraday RRS strategy does **not** beat SPY buy-and-hold — not even gross of
   costs, over its own tuning window. Do not deploy capital to it expecting
   alpha, and do not raise risk/leverage to chase the "100X" target.
2. **Abandon the "signal service revenue" path** as a route to the dollar goal.
   Selling signals from a strategy that trails buy-and-hold is not profitability.
3. **Next operator run should make the comparison the engine's primary output**
   and extend it: (a) calibrate `ROUND_TRIP_COST_PCT` to the real broker;
   (b) add the same benchmark to `run_walkforward.py` and `run_backtest.py`;
   (c) compare against a cheap rules-based SPY exposure (e.g. SPY + 200-SMA
   trend filter), which is the real bar to clear.
4. **Escalation framing:** if, after the comparison is primary and honest, no
   change with a credible mechanism beats buy-and-hold out of sample across
   windows, recommend winding down the active-trading effort in favor of
   low-cost SPY exposure. We are close to that conclusion already; one more
   run with the measurement wired in should confirm or refute it.
