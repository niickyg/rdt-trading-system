## Conclusion (honest)

On the mission's own terms — positive P&L net of honest costs, **beating SPY
buy-and-hold** — the RRS strategy as built **fails, and the failure is structural**,
not a tuning bug. The system's own docs derive the ~7% ceiling ("signal-limited, not
capital-limited") and the ML layer is conceded to be advisory-only (exit predictor
43% ≈ random). The `*_100X_*.md` docs try to close the gap with **leverage** and
**non-trading revenue (a signal service)** — both out-of-mandate: leverage multiplies
a sub-SPY edge, and selling signals is not the bot being profitable.

I did **not** ship any strategy/risk/execution change. There was no honest,
in-mandate change available that would move a negative-alpha strategy past SPY, and
inventing one to look busy would violate the honesty override in the mandate.
