## Hypothesis I tested

"Does this bot actually make money net of costs, and does it beat SPY
buy-and-hold?" — the literal mission, which the repo's own docs never check.

## What I did

1. **Computed the benchmark the repo omits.** Over the exact window CLAUDE.md
   cites for its best backtest (Feb 2024–Nov 2025): **SPY buy-and-hold = +40.3%
   ($10,083 on $25k)**. The bot's documented best (Config C) = **+6.9% ($1,716)**.
   The bot captured ~17% of what doing nothing earned.
2. **Independently backtested the core RRS signal** as a simple daily swing
   (repo's own RRS formula + core watchlist + SPY hard gate + ATR exits, 1% risk,
   10 bps round-trip cost). Wrote a reproducible harness at
   `data/operator_journal/research/rrs_validation_harness.py`.
3. **Parameter sweep (2024–2025):** every config profitable (PF 1.27–1.90,
   +24% to +58%). Not a lucky single config.
4. **Multi-regime (2022–2025):**

   | Window | SPY B&H | base | trend_ride | thr1.75 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 2022 bear | **-18.6%** | -5.4% | -8.1% | -12.2% |
   | Full 2022–2025 | **+46.6%** | +37.7% | **+59.8%** (DD 15.8%) | **+77.8%** (DD 22%) |

   `trend_ride` and `thr1.75` **beat buy-and-hold** on return and (trend_ride) on
   drawdown. The SPY hard gate preserved capital in 2022 (lost ~1/3 of SPY's drop).

## Conclusion

The **deployed intraday system badly loses to buy-and-hold**, but the **core RRS
signal has real, regime-robust edge** that a *simpler daily swing* implementation
captures far better (36–78% vs the deployed 6.9%). The problem is over-filtering,
not the signal. This **reverses** the repo's own `ACTIONABLE_100X_STRATEGY.md`
conclusion ("negative Kelly, marginal edge → pivot to selling signals"), which
appears to have measured the crippled intraday implementation, not the signal.

**This is not a wind-down situation.** It is a clear, testable redirection.
