## Assessment (this session)

Reconstructed the bot's history from its own docs (`WEALTH_OPTIMIZATION.md`,
`WEALTH_STRATEGY_100X.md`, `ACTIONABLE_100X_STRATEGY.md`, `CLAUDE.md`) and then
generated fresh primary evidence rather than trusting those docs.

Two material problems with every headline result in the repo:

1. **Backtests model zero transaction costs.** `grep -rniE
   'commission|slippage|fee|spread'` over `backtesting/*.py` → nothing. The
   engine applies no friction on entry or exit.
2. **No SPY buy-and-hold benchmark** is ever computed — the mandate's actual
   definition of success.

I wrote `scripts/honest_benchmark_backtest.py` to fix both: one contiguous,
compounding backtest over the trailing ~2 years with IBKR-like costs ($0.005/sh,
min $1) + 5 bps/side slippage, benchmarked against SPY buy-and-hold over the
identical dates. Data via the repo's own DataLoader (yfinance, fresh through
2026-10-13). All 30 watchlist stocks + SPY + 11 ETFs + VIX loaded.

**Results — window 2024-12-06 → 2026-10-13 (463 trading days, ~1.85y), $25k:**

| Config | Gross | Costs | Net total | Net annualized |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline (no filters) | +7.20% | $807 | **+3.97%** | **+2.13%** |
| RDT-filtered ("best") | +10.12% | $1,092 | **+5.75%** | **+3.07%** |
| **SPY buy-and-hold** | — | — | **+30.96%** | **+15.69%** |

**The strategy underperformed SPY buy-and-hold by ~25 percentage points.** Costs
roughly halved the gross edge. This independently corroborates (and, with costs,
worsens) the repo's own ~3.4% annualized figure.

Caveats I will not hide: the strategy is only partially invested (≤8 risk-sized
positions) while SPY buy-and-hold is fully invested in a bull market, so some of
the gap is exposure/beta rather than skill. The cost model is generous. This is
one bull-regime window. See `POST_MORTEM_RRS.md` for the full caveat list.
