## What I changed (focused, reviewable, safety-neutral)

**`backtesting/engine_enhanced.py`** — added a configurable transaction-cost model:
- New constructor params: `commission_per_share=0.005`, `min_commission=1.0`,
  `slippage_bps=5.0` (set all to 0 to reproduce legacy frictionless behavior).
- Helpers `_apply_slippage(price, side)` (buys fill higher, sells lower) and
  `_commission(shares)` (per-share with order-minimum floor).
- Applied adversely at **all three fill points**: `_enter_position`,
  `_scale_out`, `_close_position`. P&L is now fully net of costs; entry commission
  is pre-charged so `trade.pnl` is truthful.
- Tracks `total_commission` / `total_slippage_cost`.

**`scripts/run_walkforward_v2.py`** — made the filtered engines' custom entry
(`_enter_position_filtered`) cost-aware too, added a header disclosure, and an
"ESTIMATED TRANSACTION COSTS" summary block so a reviewer sees the drag directly.

**`tests/test_backtest_costs.py`** (new) — 8 offline unit tests for slippage
direction, commission floor, per-fill deduction, zero-cost equivalence, and the
capital-conservation invariant (`final_capital == start + net trade.pnl`).
