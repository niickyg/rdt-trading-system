## Caveats (all favor the strategy, and it still loses)

- **No costs modeled.** 274+ trades at PF ~1.2–1.4 is exactly where realistic
  slippage + commissions erase the gross edge. Honest costs → worse.
- **Hindsight watchlist.** 30 current mega-caps (AAPL, NVDA, META, …) =
  survivorship bias in the strategy's favor.
- **No cross-window compounding / path risk.** Each window gets a fresh $25K.
- The data span happened to be a strong SPY market, but that is the point: the
  mandate's benchmark *is* SPY, and the bot failed to capture it while taking on
  its own drawdowns.

So the conclusion is robust: **removing the caveats would only widen the gap.**

## What the repo gets right (so this isn't all negative)

- The RDT filter stack genuinely improves trade quality over baseline (best
  profit factor, best risk-adjusted of the three; "98% filtered" is real).
- The codebase is careful on security/infra (per CLAUDE.md audits).
- The problem is not code quality. It's that there is **no demonstrated edge over
  the benchmark** — a strategy problem, not an engineering one.

## Decision

Per MANDATE protocol step 4: **(c) No edge, and no specific credible untested
hypothesis in hand this run.** Therefore:

- **Do NOT deploy capital.** Keep paper-only. Do not increase risk.
- **Explicitly reject** the `ACTIONABLE_100X_STRATEGY.md` / `WEALTH_*` prescriptions
  (3% per-trade risk, 3x leveraged ETFs). They sit on a strategy whose own math
  shows a negative Kelly; leverage on a negative edge accelerates ruin. Left the

- **Flag for the human maintainer:** this is currently a research project, not a
  trading system. Continue only if a genuine, testable alpha hypothesis exists.

## Recommendations for the next run (in priority order)

1. **Confirm the benchmark gap holds with costs.** Add a per-trade cost model
   (commission + slippage in bps/ATR) to `run_walkforward_vs_spy.py` and re-run.
   If RDT's ~5% becomes ~2% or negative, the case for wind-down is closed.
2. **Test for *risk-adjusted* value, not just absolute.** Compute Sharpe and max
   drawdown for each config vs SPY. If the strategy is genuinely low-drawdown and
   uncorrelated, there may be a narrow market-neutral case — but current evidence
   (drawdowns during SPY rallies) suggests not. Disprove it cleanly.
3. **One honest alpha hypothesis, or wind down.** If someone has a specific,
   testable reason to expect edge (e.g. intraday RRS on a non-survivor universe,
   a specific regime filter), test *exactly that* and nothing else. Otherwise
   recommend formal wind-down to the maintainer. **Stop adding features.**
4. **Do not touch the SaaS/signal-service surface** until there is an edge to
   sell. Selling signals with no demonstrated edge is the worst outcome.
